Alpha 8.0 update

Levels 1 to 5 now use the new lounge gameplay background. The old Coins and Hearts gameplay tabs are removed. Moves and Target information now fit the redesigned top panels.

The playable board is taller, with 8 cells across and 11 cells down to match the new artwork. Match checking, cascades, drops, reshuffles, boosters and Level 5 handbag blockers were updated for the expanded grid. Gameplay icons were resized to stay inside their cells.

Included

Five playable levels, match-3 swapping, cascades, targets, boosters, Disco pieces, Level 5 Couture Vault handbags, Road Map navigation, daily Events, saved progress, sound effects and lounge music.
