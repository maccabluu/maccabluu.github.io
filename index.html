WAM BAM THE GAME

Match. Progress. Make it yours.

Wam Bam Thank U Mam is BluStudio's colourful match-3 puzzle game. Each level gives you a different target to complete, with new board layouts, obstacles, special pieces and challenges as you move through the world. Match pieces, create rockets and bombs, clear locks and work towards the level goal before your moves run out.

Different Level Goals

Levels use different targets, icons and board layouts so the game keeps changing as you progress.

Power Pieces

Create special pieces such as the Lipstick Rocket and Glam Bomb, then combine them for bigger clears.

The Bam Lounge

Complete levels, earn decoration stars and upgrade your own Bam Lounge with new features and room changes.

THE JOURNEY

Comic-book levels.

Travel through Wam Bam's colourful world, including Crater Wam, Red Shoe District, Diamond Mines, Glamour Point, Starfall Canyon, Boom Bay and more. Each area brings a different look and gives the game a changing sense of place as you work through the map.

POWER PIECES

Rocket. Bomb. Locks.

Learn the Lipstick Rocket, Glam Bomb and Pink Locks before the level starts. Match larger groups to create special pieces, combine them for stronger effects and use them at the right time to finish tougher goals.

MAKE IT YOURS

The Bam Lounge.

The Bam Lounge gives your progress somewhere to show. Finish levels, earn decoration stars and build out the room over time. Upgrades include the neon sign, velvet seating, jukebox and dance floor, with the room changing as each upgrade is added.

ABOUT THE TEAM
BluStudio

BluStudio is a small indie game team focused on creating colourful, personality-led games with a clear visual identity. We are building our projects step by step, testing ideas, improving features and shaping each release around the experience we want players to have.

Wam Bam Thank U Mam is our main project. It brings together match-3 gameplay, themed worlds, special pieces, progression and the Bam Lounge upgrade system. We are continuing to add levels, features and polish as the game grows.

More from BluStudio will follow as the team continues developing new ideas and expanding its games.
